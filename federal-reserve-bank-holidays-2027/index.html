Federal Reserve Bank Holidays 2027: Full List and Settlement Notes

The Federal Reserve System observes 11 holidays a year (schedule K.8). In 2027, two of them fall on a Saturday, and under the Fed's rule the Reserve Banks stay open on the Friday before. So there are 9 weekday closures to plan around.

The Saturday and Sunday rules

When a K.8 holiday falls on a Sunday, the Reserve Banks close on the following Monday. That is why Independence Day 2027 is observed on Monday, 5 July. When a holiday falls on a Saturday, the Reserve Banks do not close on the Friday before. In 2027 that affects Juneteenth (19 June) and Christmas Day (25 December). Many employers and some markets do take the Friday off, so a payroll calendar and a settlement calendar can disagree on those two Fridays.

What a Fed holiday stops

On a closure day, ACH files are not settled, Fedwire funds transfers do not run, and check clearing through the Reserve Banks pauses. Card payments, ATMs and online banking usually keep working, because they don't depend on the Reserve Banks being open. A transfer started on a holiday normally begins settling on the next business day.

Long weekends to watch in 2027

  • New Year's Day on a Friday makes a three-day weekend; the first settlement day of 2027 is Monday, 4 January.
  • MLK Day, Washington's Birthday, Memorial Day, Labor Day and Columbus Day are all Mondays, so the Friday before each one is the last settlement day.
  • Thanksgiving week has one closure, Thursday 25 November. Friday 26 November is a normal Reserve Bank business day.

Banks are not the same as the stock market

The NYSE calendar overlaps with the Fed's but is not identical. For example, the NYSE closes on Good Friday (26 March 2027), which is not a Fed holiday, and it stays open on Columbus Day and Veterans Day, which are.
